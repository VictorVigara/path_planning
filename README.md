# Contact Navigation Experiment

The following video demonstrates the result of a **contact navigation experiment** involving a drone operating in an environment with thin wires. In this scenario:

- The onboard **LiDAR sensor** is **unable to detect** thin wire obstacles.
- As a result, a **collision occurs**, which is successfully **detected by the collision platform**.
- Upon detecting the impact, the system allows the drone to **recover from the collision**.
- The drone then **re-plans its trajectory** and ultimately finds a **collision-free path** to continue its navigation.

This experiment highlights the importance of contact-based sensing as a complementary approach to conventional perception systems.
